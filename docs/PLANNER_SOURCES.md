# Kerala planner reference notes

Reviewed 19 September 2026 against Kerala Tourism, the state tourism department.
These references establish the locations and activity types used in the expanded
starter plans. They do not verify live availability, bookings, weather, access or
prices. Food breaks and time allocations are Roamly suggestions, not venue offers.

Silent Valley is one guided excursion with a substantial travel and visit block;
its viewpoints are not scheduled as independent tours on successive days. Its
programme information includes older official material, so the UI requires users
to confirm reporting time and arrangements with the reserve. The planner leaves
extra days open after its curated stops run out, rather than repeating visits.

Scheduled costs remain estimated allocations from the user's daily budget. Open
days have no invented activity charges; accommodation and travel to the destination
remain excluded from all activity allocations.
